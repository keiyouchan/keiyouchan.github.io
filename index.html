Bio

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) in EICP Lab.

Prior to HKUST-GZ, I worked as a research assistant at the London School of Economics and completed M.A. in Industrial Economics at the Southwestern University of Finance and Economics and B.A. in Economics at Huaqiao University.

My main research interests sit at Energy Economics, Environmental Economics, and Industrial Organization.
